Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian layered pasta dish known for its comforting combination of rich meat sauce, creamy béchamel, and melted cheese. It’s hearty, deeply flavorful, and perfect for gatherings or cozy dinners.

Each layer of tender pasta sheets is spread with a robust tomato-based meat sauce, creamy béchamel, and generous amounts of mozzarella and parmesan. When baked, the flavors meld into a golden, bubbling perfection that’s both indulgent and satisfying.

Ingredients

For the Meat Sauce (Ragù):

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 500 g ground beef (or a mix of beef and pork)
  • 400 g canned tomatoes (crushed or pureed)
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon dried basil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

For the Béchamel Sauce:

  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups milk (preferably warm)
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • A pinch of nutmeg (optional)

For Assembly:

  • 9–12 lasagna sheets (pre-cooked or oven-ready)
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • ½ cup grated parmesan cheese
  • Fresh basil or parsley for garnish

Steps

  1. Heat olive oil in a large pan. Sauté onions until soft, then add garlic and cook for a minute. Add the minced meat and cook until browned. Stir in tomato paste, crushed tomatoes, oregano, basil, salt, and pepper. Simmer uncovered for 20–25 minutes until thickened.
  2. In another pan, melt butter over medium heat. Stir in flour and cook for 1 minute. Gradually add warm milk, whisking continuously to avoid lumps. Cook until smooth and slightly thick. Season with salt, pepper, and a pinch of nutmeg.
  3. Set your oven to 180°C (350°F).
  4. In a baking dish, spread a thin layer of meat sauce. Place lasagna sheets over it. Add another layer of meat sauce, followed by béchamel, then sprinkle mozzarella. Repeat layers until all ingredients are used, finishing with béchamel and a generous topping of mozzarella and parmesan.
  5. Cover the dish with foil and bake for 25 minutes. Remove the foil and bake another 15–20 minutes until the top is golden and bubbly.
  6. Allow the lasagna to rest for 10–15 minutes before cutting. Garnish with fresh basil or parsley.
