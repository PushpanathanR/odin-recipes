Puliogare

Description

Puliogare, also known as tamarind rice, is a tangy, spicy, and aromatic South Indian rice dish traditionally offered in temples and made for festive or travel meals. It combines cooked rice with a rich tamarind-based paste infused with roasted spices, curry leaves, and a nutty crunch from peanuts or sesame.

The dish’s beauty lies in its balance — the sourness of tamarind, the warmth of red chilies, and the earthy aroma of mustard seeds and curry leaves blend into a flavorful comfort meal that stays fresh for hours without spoiling.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice (preferably cooled)
  • 2 tablespoons thick tamarind pulp
  • tablespoons sesame oil or gingelly oil
  • 1 teaspoon mustard seeds
  • 2 dried red chilies
  • 10–12 curry leaves
  • 2 tablespoons roasted peanuts (optional: roasted cashews)
  • 1 tablespoon chana dal (split Bengal gram)
  • 1 tablespoon urad dal (split black gram)
  • >¼ teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 2 teaspoons jaggery (adjust to taste)
  • Salt to taste

Steps

  1. Cook and cool the rice:
    Cook the rice so each grain is separate. Spread it on a plate to cool completely.
  2. Prepare the tamarind extract:
    Soak the tamarind pulp in ½ cup warm water for 10 minutes and squeeze out the thick extract.
  3. Roast the masala (if making fresh):
    In a dry pan, roast coriander seeds, sesame seeds, methi, red chilies, and peppercorns till aromatic. Cool and grind to a fine powder.
  4. Make the tempering:
    Heat sesame oil in a pan. Add mustard seeds, red chilies, chana dal, urad dal, and curry leaves. Sauté until the dals turn golden brown.
  5. Add tamarind mixture:
    Pour in the tamarind extract, add turmeric, salt, and jaggery. Simmer until it thickens slightly and the raw tamarind smell disappears.
  6. Add roasted masala:
    Stir in the puliogare masala and cook for another 2–3 minutes. The mixture should form a thick, glossy paste.
  7. Combine with rice:
    Add the cooled rice and roasted peanuts to the pan. Gently mix until the rice is evenly coated with the tamarind paste.
  8. Rest before serving:
    Let it sit covered for 15–20 minutes before serving — this helps the flavors deepen.
